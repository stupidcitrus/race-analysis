File info
Every output contains only the kept data (green in the chart) and uses the export rate, knots option and columns chosen above (except the manoeuvres CSV).

Excel MATLAB-style — to redo the MATLAB analysis. For each race, 3 files named as in your script: name_N.xlsx (header + Time, Race, Segment, Leg, all log columns, DeltaBS), TWS_TABname_N.xlsx and TWS_RUDname_N.xlsx (two columns, no header). With 3 races that is 9 files: the browser may ask permission for multiple downloads. N starts from "Number of the first race". Offline: same files as CSV.

Single Excel — one file to archive or share, with the sheets Summary (duration, kept time, manoeuvres, mean TWS and BS), R1/R2/R3 (clean data of each race), Manoeuvres and Manual intervals. Needs internet.

Clean CSV — all races in one file for Python, R, Excel. "Race" gives the race, "Segment" numbers the continuous clean pieces, "Leg" U/R/D. With rate "original", no knots conversion and all columns ticked, it copies the log rows unchanged with 4 columns appended. The CSV of a single race is downloaded with ⤓ CSV in the Summary.

Manoeuvres CSV — no log data: the list of detected manoeuvres with times, type, detail, window removed, whether applied (1/0) and race. Useful to count them or to check the cuts.

File names — by default they come from the log name. Choose "custom" in "Output file name" to use your own name (e.g. Cowes2026_ → Cowes2026_1.xlsx, Cowes2026_clean.csv).

How it works
Races. If the log has a Race Timer, each start is the moment the countdown crosses zero (false starts and sync tests, where the timer resets within a few minutes, are ignored). Otherwise |TWA| and speed are averaged and every moment is classified as stopped / upwind / reach / downwind; long enough upwind and downwind stretches are "legs", and consecutive legs with short gaps form a candidate race. A race ends at the end of its last leg, at a stop, or, if the last leg is abnormally long, where the boat leaves the race area (flagged ⚠). Dock-out, sail home, waiting and short practice runs stay out.
Tacks and gybes. As in the MATLAB script: find the TWA sign change and remove the [before, after] window around it (MATLAB used 20 samples before and 40 after; here they are seconds, independent of the sample rate). Sign changes close together are grouped: odd count = manoeuvre, even count = flicker around 0°/180°.
Mark roundings. Upwind→downwind or downwind→upwind transition of the averaged |TWA|. Penalties. Cumulative heading rotation above the threshold in a short time.
Order. (races + Add) − manoeuvres − Remove − excluded points of sail, then + Force.
