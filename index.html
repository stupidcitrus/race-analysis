Onboard start data

From Charisma's instrument log (10 Hz), not from the tracks above. The owner updates it with "Load log".
TWS (kn) = mean over the first minute after the gun. BSP min / max (kn) = lowest and highest boat speed from 60 s before to 90 s after the gun, with the time from the gun when they occurred. Deep angle (°) = how far the boat bore away in the last 60 s before the gun, compared with its mean heading in the 90 s after the start (based on heading). Ratio (m/s) = distance from the start line divided by time to/from the gun: 1 min before the start and 1.5 min after. DtL = distance behind the start line (m) at 60 s, 30 s and at the gun, from the boat's start-line data; negative = over the line.

How the data is calculated
  • Tracks are resampled to 1 second. SOG and COG are derived from positions (difference over ±2 s) and then smoothed; COG uses a circular mean so it stays correct around 0°/360°.
  • Marks are detected automatically from the turning points along the wind axis; each mark's position is the average of the boats' rounding points.
  • If you don't enter TWD and start time, the app estimates them from the tracks (main course axis and the fleet accelerating upwind): always check the proposed values.
  • The start line is approximated: perpendicular to the leg-1 axis, level with the most advanced boat at the gun.
